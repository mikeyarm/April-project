AUTHOR

Overview

Authors and writers create content for different media, using their skills to educate, entertain, and inspire. They select topics that will engage their listeners and create such content as fiction, nonfiction, biographies, scripts, and copy to sell for print, broadcast, and internet publications. In order to deliver their writing as precise and compelling as possible, they like to use extensive research. Writers send their manuscripts to editors and clients and collaborate to edit the content for final publication. The majority are self-employed and can write from anywhere with a computer. Apart from storytelling, writers have significant impacts on society by influencing cultural values, encouraging critical thinking, and advancing social discourse. Their writing informs people about other viewpoints, fosters empathy, and enables meaningful cultural discussion and reform.


Michael Urbaniak
