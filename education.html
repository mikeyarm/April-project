Education

A college degree in English, communications, or journalism is generally required for a full-time position as a writer or author. Northwestern University, University of Texas at Austin, University of Southern California, Boston University, and University of Missouri, and more offer one or more of these programs. However, you don't necessarily need to have a degree or a big, fancy education. All you really need to do is write a book that publishers will take.

Michael Urbaniak
